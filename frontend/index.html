Structured Workflow

How Complaints Are Processed

Complaints follow an automated, objective priority resolution lifecycle.

1
Citizen Submission

Citizen submits complaint specifying location, description, severity, and number of affected residents.

2
Priority Queuing

Backend evaluates Severity (Critical > High), Affected Count, and Arrival Order into Department PriorityQueue.

3
Capacity Assignment

Assigned immediately if capacity permits; otherwise held in priority waiting queue until slots release.

4
Field Resolution

Engineers complete on-site repair, enter resolution notes, and the citizen receives transparent tracking updates.

Dedicated Municipal Teams

Four Specialized Operating Departments

To maintain service reliability, Makkal Nagar operates specialized teams managed with capacity limits to prevent worker overload and ensure thorough field resolution.

Water Supply Department
Handles domestic connections, valves, and water quality testing.
Roads and Infrastructure Department
Manages asphalt resurfacing, pothole filling, and civil works.
Streetlight and Electrical Department
Maintains overhead grids, timers, LED fixtures, and distribution boxes.
Sanitation and Waste Management Department
Coordinates solid waste segregation, dumper logistics, and drainage hygiene.
